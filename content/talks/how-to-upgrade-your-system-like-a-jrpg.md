Follow me and enter the world of VistaTorn, a bleak and dreary country full of inadequate search, elongated wait-times, hardcoded permissions and no observability as far as the eye can see (which is not very far). In this place rises a hero, a visionary, a beacon of innovation... and that hero is YOU!

Learn how to navigate the murky waters of search, with no semantics or vector databases in sight. What tools should you implement to upgrade and conquer these streams of data? Within the castle, work to improve the throughput of services and investigate any and all latencies that arise through instrumentation of standardized and trusted observability tools. Join me on this journey to conquer your stack and save VistaTorn!
