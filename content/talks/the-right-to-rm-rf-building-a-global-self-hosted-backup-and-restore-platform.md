The cloud is just someone else's computer but you data is your own. In this talk I will discuss common misconceptions about today's Internet that leave people unknowingly giving up their right to privacy, like Third Party Doctrine and Social Media, how to protect their data at home with self hosted infrastructure using ZFS and Samba 3 and how to keep access to your things secure even when crossing borders.


I work in cybersecurity and travel with my devices. I've been through countless border crossings, airport inspections and in insecure work environments where I must protect my data while maintaining minimal downtime. In this talk, I will demonstrate my self hosted infrastructure that allows me to travel in peace knowing that my network is secure, my data is intact, my privacy is maintained and my downtime is minimal.
