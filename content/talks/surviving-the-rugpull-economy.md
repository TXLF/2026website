The story of open source and the Internet is a story of dystopia timelines that didn't happen.  We avoided the Clipper Chip, key escrow.  And the Fritz Chip, DRM mandates.  Sure, threats to security, and DRM lock-in, are still there, but thanks to a lot of hard work, they're manageable.  Today's biggest threat, though, isn't coming from outside forces like governments or big copyright holders. Our ongoing collapse of trust and rise of crime is rooted in some  good ideas from open source, gone wrong.


It's not just social media scams and deepfakes.  Dishonest business practices from the sketchier corners of the Internet have gone mainstream.  We're in a rugpull economy now.  Let's figure out where we went wrong and how to get back on track.
