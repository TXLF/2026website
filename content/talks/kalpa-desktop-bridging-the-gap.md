Kalpa desktop is a desktop linux offering, based on the concepts of transactional and atomic updates, that bridges the gap between the flexibility (and sometimes chaos) of a traditional fully read-write Linux Distribution, and the stability of image based read-only systems.


Kalpa leverages btrfs snapshots, so your running system is never in danger, all updates being done in an offline manner.


Combined with the rolling release nature of openSUSE Tumbleweed, you've got some of the freshest software out there, without having to deal with some of the bumpier parts of being on a rolling release.


This workshop will touch on Kalpa and how we do things, a bit of a contrast and compare with other projects in our space, and questions are *very* welcome.
