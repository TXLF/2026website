What if your development environment didn't have to live on your laptop? In this hands-on workshop, you'll explore how Coder enables software teams to build, test, and collaborate using cloud development environments. You'll get practical experience working in a remote workspace, integrating AI coding agents into your development workflow, and exploring how code review fits into the process.


Whether you're a developer looking to simplify your setup or a platform engineer exploring more consistent development environments for your team, this session will show you how Coder brings development workflows together without sacrificing flexibility or your preferred tools.


Come ready to get hands-on, write code, and see what development beyond the laptop looks like.
