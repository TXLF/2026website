Every sysadmin knows the five stages of outage grief, and they all end at DNS. Yet for something that runs the entire internet, a clear and approachable explanation of it is surprisingly hard to find. In this talk, Jonathan will walk through a little history of DNS, what nameservers actually do, what TTLs are, and everything from caching to record types to email. Come learn the backbone technology you blame every day, so next time you can say "it was DNS" with confidence.
