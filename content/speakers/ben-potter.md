Ben Potter is a Product Manager focused on Open Source and Developer Relations at Coder. An engineer at heart, Ben has spent much of his career building, experimenting, and developing on remote machines. At Coder, he brings that hands-on experience to helping developers and teams adopt cloud development environments, streamline their workflows, and build with greater flexibility. He's passionate about open source, developer tooling, and making great development experiences accessible to everyone.
