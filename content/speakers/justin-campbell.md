I started tinkering with computers when I was 11 with Ubuntu's Wubi installer alongside Windows XP. 3 years later I started an IT consulting business providing managed networking and virtualization services to small and medium business. That took me to Kenya to deploy Raspberry Pis on car batteries for hospitals and the African Great Lakes region to develop custom communications systems for peace keeping teams. After selling that business to pay for college, I got my degree and have worked in a variety of jobs including lead engineer migrating a large research university's internal tooling from Perl on Solaris to Python on all platforms, principal engineer for a Nebula communications platform for threat detection and deep packet inspection at scale and now as the Vice President of Engineering for a platform hardening tool.  With any luck, I can spend the next many years sharing my knowledge and following my passion for educating other about digital privacy and delivering tools to help other protect themselves.
