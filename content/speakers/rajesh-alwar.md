Rajesh Alwar is a Systems Engineer with a background in cloud infrastructure and enterprise-scale systems, including designing on-premises edge compute solutions. Outside of work, he builds open source tooling for personal data analysis and automation from local LLM experimentation with Ollama to Python-based stock market scanners and Strava analytics pipelines. Rajesh is passionate about local-first, privacy-respecting AI and enjoys exploring how open source tools on Linux can replace cloud-dependent workflows without sacrificing capability.
