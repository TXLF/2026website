Autumn Nash is a software engineer at Microsoft Azure, open source contributor, speaker, and community leader who explores what it means to build excellent engineers in an age of rapid technological change. Her work sits at the intersection of Linux, open source, AI, and engineering culture, where she helps teams think beyond tools to the judgment, stewardship, and collaboration that create resilient engineering organizations.

At Microsoft, Autumn works on Azure Linux with a focus on Linux security and open source collaboration. Previously, she was a software engineer at AWS working on Amazon Corretto and contributing to OpenJDK, after serving as a NoSQL Solutions Architect helping customers build distributed systems at scale.

Beyond her engineering work, Autumn co-hosts the Fork Around and Find Out podcast, serves as Education Board Chair for MilSpouse Coders, and founded The Little Guild, a nonprofit dedicated to strengthening local communities. Whether contributing to open source, mentoring engineers, or speaking at conferences, she's passionate about leaving the engineering profession stronger than she found it.
