Mike is the Founder of Gluu, a cybersecurity infrastructure software vendor. He is the founder and BD of the Janssen Project, a self-funded Linux Foundation project to provide a home for open source identity and access management infrastructure. He hosts the “Identerati Office Hours” Livestream twice a week, which features discussions on all topics digital identity and application security. He hosted a podcast from 2018-2024 called “Open Source Underdogs”, which featured many leaders in open source business community. Mike resides in Austin TX with family and pigeons.
