Jonathan is the Infrastructure Team Lead for AlmaLinux.  He has been consuming open source and building infrastructure in the web hosting industry for over 20 years and actively contributing to open source for roughly the past 6 within the AlmaLinux, Fedora, and EPEL communities, among others.  Outside of technology he is an avid hunter and conservationist, boating and water sports enthusiast, member of the United States Coast Guard Auxiliary, and instrument-rated private pilot.
